## What to explain

- `app.analyze_uploads` runs original OCR independently of the image pipeline. It stores both results but passes one selected text to `classify_document` and `extract_fields`. The diagrams separate this per-file work from the final per-type decision.
- `analyze_readiness` builds detected and usable sets for the selected checklist. With four detected types and three usable types, 100 × 4/4 gives 100% coverage, while 100 × 3/4 gives 75% readiness. A missing name does not remove the type's detection.
- The OCR comparison uses eight fictional images representing four source layouts and four related derivatives. Original and default pooled CER both equal zero. The frozen evidence supports the tie and the measured failures of some alternatives on these inputs. Accuracy on unfamiliar layouts remains unmeasured.
